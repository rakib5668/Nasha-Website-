NASHA Community Rules

Keep NASHA friendly, safe, and enjoyable for everyone. By joining, you agree to follow these rules.

01 · Respect Everyone

Treat every member with respect. Bullying, harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are not allowed.

02 · No Toxicity or Drama

Avoid unnecessary arguments, provoking others, threats, and drama. Keep conversations mature and friendly.

03 · No Spam or Flooding

Do not spam messages, repeat content, abuse mentions, or flood text and voice channels.

04 · Keep Content Appropriate

Do not share explicit, illegal, disturbing, or otherwise inappropriate content. Follow Discord's Terms of Service.

05 · No Advertising Without Permission

Do not advertise servers, social accounts, products, or services without staff approval.

06 · Protect Privacy

Never share anyone's private information, messages, photos, or personal details without permission.

07 · Use Channels Correctly

Post in the right channels and follow each channel's topic and pinned instructions.

08 · Voice Chat Etiquette

Do not deafen others with loud audio, mic spam, soundboards, or disruptive behavior.

09 · No Impersonation or Scams

Do not impersonate members or staff, scam users, distribute malicious links, or attempt phishing.

10 · Listen to Staff

Follow reasonable moderator instructions. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it calmly in private with staff.

11 · No Rule Evasion

Do not use alternate accounts or other methods to evade mutes, bans, or moderation actions.

12 · Follow Discord Guidelines

All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Staff may warn, mute, kick, or ban members depending on the severity or repetition of a violation. These rules may be updated when needed.
